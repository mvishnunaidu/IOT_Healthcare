Problem Statement

Traditional healthcare monitoring systems suffer from critical delays in data processing and emergency response. Relying heavily on centralized cloud servers for decision-making increases latency during medical emergencies, which can be fatal.

Objective

To enable real-time patient monitoring using integrated IoT sensors and edge computing. This minimizes latency, ensures data privacy, and triggers instant life-saving alerts before data is even sent to the cloud.

Edge Computing

By processing critical patient data locally at the edge, the system achieves faster emergency detection and reduced latency, crucial for immediate medical interventions without waiting for cloud round-trips.
